## Common Patterns Identified

### Navigation Elements
Most FM websites include:
- About Us / Who We Are
- Services (often split into categories)
- Sectors / Industries
- Projects / Case Studies
- News / Insights
- Careers
- Contact

### Services Categories
Common service groupings:
1. **Hard Services** - Technical, mechanical, electrical
2. **Soft Services** - Cleaning, security, catering
3. **Integrated FM** - Combined service offerings

### Design Patterns
- Hero sections with strong imagery
- Service cards/grids
- Sector-specific landing pages
- Client testimonials
- Accreditations/certifications display
- News/insights blog

### Call-to-Actions
- "Get in Touch"
- "Contact Us"
- "Learn More"
- "Request a Quote"

## Recommendations for QualFM

Based on research, QualFM should include:

### Essential Pages
1. Home (hero, services overview, sectors, CTA)
2. About Us (company story, values, team)
3. Services (with sub-pages for each service)
4. Sectors (target industries)
5. Projects/Case Studies
6. Contact

### Services to Feature
- Facilities Management
- Building Maintenance
- HVAC Services
- Cleaning Services
- Security Solutions
- Energy Management
- Health & Safety Compliance

### Sectors to Target
- Commercial/Office
- Healthcare
- Education
- Retail
- Industrial
- Government/Public Sector

### Design Approach
- Clean, modern glassmorphic design
- Mobile-first responsive
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Clear CTAs
- Professional imagery
- Trust indicators (accreditations, testimonials)
