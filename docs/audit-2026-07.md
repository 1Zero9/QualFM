# QualFM Website Audit — July 2026

**Subject:** Rebuilt Next.js site (feat/nextjs-admin branch, pre-production)
**Weighting:** Mobile 80% / Desktop 20%
**Method:** Rendered-HTML analysis of all 8 public pages, source review, contrast computation, security-header review, structured-data validation, content audit. *Limitations: no physical-device testing; no field Core Web Vitals (site not yet in production); performance assessed from architecture and asset weights.*

---

## Executive Summary

The rebuilt site is **technically excellent and visually credible, but commercially under-armed**. The mobile-first experience passes the 10-second test: a visitor sees QualFM-branded technicians at work, a clear statement of what the company does, a tap-to-call button and certification badges — all above the fold. Technical SEO, security and performance architecture are ahead of typical SME competitors.

The gap is **proof and urgency**. At audit time the site displays **zero testimonials, zero project case studies and zero news** (all exist as unpublished drafts or empty tables). For the FM Director, Procurement Manager and Property Manager personas — the people who award contracts — the site currently *asserts* quality but *evidences* almost none. Additionally, the word "emergency" appears once on the entire site; reactive/emergency demand is the highest-intent traffic an FM company gets, and there is no path for it.

**Verdict: do not rebuild — publish proof, add an emergency path, and sharpen differentiation.** The platform (owner-editable admin, structured data, fast architecture) is exactly right; it is waiting for content that sells.

**Overall score: 7.0 / 10** — held back almost entirely by missing proof content, not by design or engineering.

### High

| # | Finding | Evidence | Impact |
|---|---------|----------|--------|

| H2 | **/news and /projects have no H1** (heading starts at h2); footer skips h2/h3 → h4. | Rendered HTML | Accessibility (WCAG 1.3.1) and SEO heading hierarchy. |
| H3 | **"25+ Years sector experience" adjacent to "Established in February 2025".** | Homepage trust stats vs. trust closing text | Reads as contradiction; a sceptical buyer may perceive inflation. Reframe: "25 years' founder experience — company est. 2025". |
| H4 | **Generic differentiation.** "Quality · Compliance · Value" could be any FM firm. No quantified USP (response SLA, first-fix rate, coverage map, savings delivered). | Content audit | Scenario 4 ("comparing five providers") gives no reason to pick QualFM beyond tone. |
| H5 | **Heavy source images.** Logo PNGs 476–852 KB (van.png 852 KB, logos ~500 KB each). next/image resizes at runtime, but originals inflate optimisation cost; the logo should be an SVG (~5 KB). | `du` of /public/images | LCP risk on 4G; brand asset shipped at 100× necessary weight. |

### Low

- Empty-state messages on /news and /projects are good UX but currently *the* content — resolves with C1.
- `eval()` CSP console warning is dev-only; production unaffected.
- Breadcrumbs exist as schema only, no visible UI (acceptable at this site size).

### What already works (keep)

- **Mobile hero**: branded crew photo, headline, tap-to-call, cert badges — best-in-class for SME FM.
- **Technical SEO**: per-page metadata, dynamic sitemap, robots, LocalBusiness/Service/FAQPage/NewsArticle/Breadcrumb JSON-LD. Ahead of most competitors in the sector.
- **Security**: strict CSP, security headers, rate-limited + honeypotted contact API, magic-link admin. 
- **Performance architecture**: server components, minimal client JS, next/image everywhere, self-hosted font, edge-cacheable pages.
- **Owner self-service admin**: hero, news, projects, testimonials, FAQs, clients all owner-editable with graceful fallbacks — content velocity without developer bottleneck.
- **Colour system & consistency**: brand palette applied consistently; alternating section rhythm on mobile; components coherent.

## Roadmap

### Quick wins (< 2 hours each)

1. **Publish the seeded testimonials** after Richard verifies them (admin → Testimonials). *Zero code.*
2. **Add 2–3 projects** with photos via admin → Jobs. *Zero code.*
3. Fix duplicate homepage H1 (demote the hidden variant to `<p>` with matching styles).
4. Add H1s to /news and /projects; fix footer heading levels.
5. Reframe "25+ years" stat → "25 yrs founder experience".
6. Convert logo to SVG (or ≤ 20 KB PNG); compress van.png and hero JPEG.
7. Darken forest-green on soft-green surfaces to pass 4.5:1.
8. Add GBP/LinkedIn URLs to footer + `sameAs` schema (once profiles exist).

**Projected effect of 1+2 alone: the two biggest trust gaps close for free.**

### Medium improvements (1–2 days each)

1. **Emergency/reactive strip** — persistent "Reactive issue? Call now — [phone]" band with a response-time promise, plus an "Emergency support" FAQ and homepage mention. *Directly addresses highest-intent traffic.*
2. **Credentials & Compliance page** — insurance summary, H&S statement, certifications, vetting/qualifications of personnel; add downloads if available. *Unblocks procurement.*
3. **Contact form upgrade** — phone field, enquiry-type select (Planned / Reactive / Fitout / Audit), response-SLA promise on the success state.
4. **Founder/team section on About** with photographs.
5. **Homepage differentiation block** — 3 quantified claims (e.g. first-response SLA, fitout ceiling €1.5m, sectors served) replacing generic statements.

### Major improvements (1–2 weeks)

1. **Sector landing pages** (Healthcare FM, Telecom & Critical Environments, Retail Fitout, Pharma) — each with sector proof, FAQ, schema; primary organic-growth lever.
2. **Case-study format upgrade** — problem → work → outcome (with numbers), 4–6 published.
3. **Review engine** — Google Business Profile reviews solicited from live clients, surfaced on site.

## Final Recommendation

1. **Rebuild?** No. The rebuild just happened and is architecturally correct. Rebuilding again would burn money to stand still.
2. **Why?** All critical gaps are *content and positioning* gaps that the new admin platform is specifically designed to close without engineering effort.
3. **Pages to keep:** all current pages.
4. **Pages to redesign:** About (add people), Contact (qualify the form).
5. **Pages to remove:** none.
6. **Pages to add:** Credentials & Compliance; 3–4 sector landing pages; (optional) Emergency Support.
7. **Ideal IA:** Home → Services (+ sector children) → Projects → About (+ Credentials) → News → Contact. Current IA is one page short of ideal.
8. **Homepage redesign?** Not needed — activate the dormant proof sections (testimonials/projects/news) and add the differentiation + emergency blocks into the existing rhythm.
9. **+30% enquiries plan:** emergency strip (+10–15% from reactive traffic), published proof (+10% conversion lift on existing traffic), qualified form + SLA promise (+5%), sector pages (+15–25% organic traffic over 2 quarters). Conservatively exceeds +30% combined.
10. **Blank canvas?** It would look substantially like what has been built — mobile-first, photo-led, owner-editable — but launched *with* 6 case studies, 8 testimonials, a compliance pack and an emergency phone line front and centre. Build the content, not another website.
